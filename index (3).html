How the Draw Works
Each player has been randomly drawn 4 teams — one from each tier. Teams are tiered by current betting odds, so everyone starts with the same expected chance of winning. Tier 4 underdogs are worth more points if they do well — so don't write anyone off!
Team Points
Points are awarded based on your team's tier — underdogs earn more for the same result.

Keeping It Fair
Wildcards fire after every match — not just certain days — so everyone has an equal chance regardless of when their teams play. Even if all your teams go out in the group stage, wildcard points keep coming in right up to the final. The World Cup winner's owner is guaranteed to win overall, but 2nd and 3rd place stays competitive throughout. 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁥󠁮󠁧󠁿⚽
